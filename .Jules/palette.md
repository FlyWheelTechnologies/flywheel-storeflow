## 2023-10-29 - [Missing ARIA Labels on Close Buttons]
**Learning:** There is a recurring pattern in the codebase of missing `aria-label` attributes on icon-only close buttons (e.g., in `ConfirmationModal.jsx`, `Layout.jsx`, `Dashboard.jsx`, and `Sales.jsx`).
**Action:** Always verify accessibility labels on interactive elements, particularly close buttons that just have an "X" or icon.
